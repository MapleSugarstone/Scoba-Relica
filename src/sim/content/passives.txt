passive roll-the-wheel "Roll the Wheel"
  text "On entry, learn a random ability modified to scale with strength and Fortuna type."
  when it takes the field:
    sound confirm
    show spin as wheel over holder, pointer spintop
    pick a random move, skip once per battle
    change picked move:
      set type fortuna
      set damage physical
      scale off strength
      tint #e8c46a
      set name "Golden {name}"
    give holder picked move as extra
    say "The wheel comes up {picked}."

passive invested "Invested"

passive house-money "Lets go gambling"
  text "At the end of each turn, learn a random 80%+ cost ability modified to scale with strength and Fortuna type. Its cost is halved."
  when a turn ends:
    sound confirm
    show spin as wheel over holder, pointer spintop
    pick a random move, costing at least 80, skip once per battle
    change picked move:
      set type fortuna
      set damage physical
      scale off strength
      tint #e8c46a
      set name "Golden {name}"
      set cost x0.5
    give holder picked move in slot 4
    say "The wheel rolled {picked}."

passive add "Add"
  text "On entry, heals all allies for [heal:add]. Whenever it casts an ability, heals the other ally Scoba for [heal:add:2], or itself if there is none."
  icon add
  when it takes the field:
    show miniadd as burst on allies on the field
    heal allies on the field 10% of source magic
  when it casts:
    show miniadd as burst on next ally scoba from holder or holder
    heal next ally scoba from holder or holder 3% of source magic

# Addiza's and Poki's Hyper-Mode passives. Each gives its Scoba a mark on entry
# and fuses with an ally carrying the other's, so whichever of the two takes the
# field in Hyper-Mode second is the one that fuses them.
passive flame-blade "Flame Blade"
  text "Sun physical abilities leave the enemy [status:seared|Seared] for [damage:seared] damage a turn for 2 turns. Stacks up to 5 times. On entry, gains [status:ultimate-multiplication|Ultimate Multiplication]. If an ally Scoba has Ultimate Addition, fuses with them into Equalizea. Equalizea picks 2 abilities a turn and has 2 HP bars and 2 mana bars. It can't block or switch out."
  icon flameblade
  fuses with ultimate-addition into equalizea
  when it lands sun physical:
    inflict seared on other
  when it takes the field:
    inflict ultimate-multiplication on holder

passive moon-cannon "Moon Cannon"
  text "Basic attacks become Moon Shots, dealing Moon magic damage off magic. On entry, gains [status:ultimate-addition|Ultimate Addition]. If an ally Scoba has Ultimate Multiplication, fuses with them into Equalizea. Equalizea picks 2 abilities a turn and has 2 HP bars and 2 mana bars. It can't block or switch out."
  icon mooncannon

# Grinkle Pylon's. It never casts: its basic attack spawns a Grinkling that
# holds its abilities and statuses, less these two, and casts them for it.
passive pylon "Pylon"
  text "Basic attacks spawn a Grinkling instead. The Grinkling has every ability and status this Scoba has except this passive and Double Spawn. Can't cast abilities."
  icon pylon
  basic attack is spawn-grinkling
  while carried:
    cannot cast spells
